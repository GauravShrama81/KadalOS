Why education needs an ontology layer now

Fragmented systems

Standards, content libraries, LMS, assessments, and analytics live in silos. Ontology creates a shared semantic backbone.

Static curriculum cycles

Most content refreshes are annual. Ontology enables continuous updates from evidence, outcomes, and policy changes.

Low explainability in AI

Black-box recommendations can’t justify instructional decisions. Ontologies provide traceable reasoning over explicit relationships.

Benefits for publishers, institutions, and learners

Schools & Universities
